# Navi privacy policy

Navi (for Mac and for iPhone) does not collect, store on a server, sell, or
share any personal data.

- **Speech.** When you talk to type, Navi records your voice only while you
  dictate. The audio is turned into text on your device (Parakeet through
  FluidAudio, or Apple's on-device speech recognition) and is not kept after
  the text is made.
- **Text.** Cleanup runs on your device (Apple Foundation Models or Navi's own
  rules). On the Mac, dictated text and captures go to a history file on your
  Mac. On iPhone, the latest text is held in a file shared only between the
  Navi app and the Navi keyboard on that iPhone.
- **Keyboard Full Access.** The Navi keyboard needs Full Access only to reach
  the Navi app through that shared file. It does not read what you type with
  other keyboards and does not use the network.
- **Network.** Navi downloads the speech model once (from Hugging Face) and,
  on the Mac, checks GitHub for updates. Neither request includes your audio,
  text, or any account information.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/donovanhiland/navi/issues.
